PROMZONA — C++23 migration prototype

This is a test native build of the port, not the full version of the original PROMZONA.
The city and the game mechanics are still simplified. It does not replace the existing game.
All assets are kept for further porting; many of them are not used yet.

Update 05.11.2026: a 49x49 city, canals and bridges, two overpasses and two tunnels.
Traffic drives on multi-level roads. Cars under a bridge and above a tunnel
keep their level; falling off a deck is subject to gravity.
Leaving a car inside a tunnel keeps the player's height.
This is not yet the current full layout of the C# game with its detailed districts and props.

Added facade materials, windows with curtains and simulated rooms,
balconies, entrances and air conditioners. Window lights change slowly.
Near details fade out at 135–185 m; the default city draw distance is 1000 m.
Facade details of the nearest blocks are loaded in the background and released far away.
Roads, terrain and the main building volumes are kept in memory within the draw distance and built in the background as you move.
Update 13.11.2026: a sky with clouds, sun, moon and stars; haze towards the horizon; reflections of the city in windows, water, puddles,
wet asphalt and on cars; ambient occlusion (SSAO). After rain the streets stay wet and slippery for several minutes.
On low quality reflections and SSAO are disabled. Launching with --quality 0|1|2 sets the quality for one session.
The city has six wanted levels, pursuit with armoured vehicles and a helicopter,
trams and trolleybuses with passengers, food kiosks and clinics.
Buses drive the streets and stop at shelters; gas service vans can be seen as well.
You can deliver a parcel, move a car, eliminate a target, hand out leaflets,
drive a loudspeaker around, do a taxi ride, deliver a patient or scout three rooftops with a drone.

Launch: Promzona.exe (Windows) or Promzona (Linux, macOS). The Content folder must stay next to it.
Requires Windows x64, Linux or macOS and a graphics driver with OpenGL 3.3. .NET is not required.

In menus, choose an item with the mouse or with the ↑/↓ arrows and Enter. Esc opens the pause menu, goes back or resumes the game. Q in the main menu quits the game.
M switches the "Radio Pustyr" track. In settings, ←/→ change the selected value. With the mouse, click the left or right half of the row.
WASD: movement. Shift: run. Space: handbrake.
F: get in/out. On foot, turn to a passer-by and press F to talk. Press again to accept an offer. R: tow a stopped car to a free road nearby. Again after 8 seconds. Damage and fire are kept. V: camera (chase, distant overhead view, top-down view).
F next to a free scooter: get on / off. WASD: ride the scooter. Space: brake.
Mouse: look around on foot. LMB: shoot. Wheel or [ and ]: weapon.
J: job log. 6: taxi, 7: patient. Stop next to the passenger and press E. E: job action. F on foot at a kiosk: buy food.
K on foot: launch / disable the drone. LMB from the drone: inspect the marked rooftop.
Space or Q / Ctrl or Z in flight: climb / descend. L: automatic helicopter landing, again to cancel. W / S on a tram: forward / backward.
At a stop, B lets you board a tram or trolleybus as a passenger. Inside, B or F requests a stop at the next station. If the vehicle is already at the platform, you get off at once.
Driving a trolleybus, stay on its route: more than three metres from the wires there is no traction.
Mouse in a tank or APC: aim the gun. LMB: fire.
J, then 8: start the next stage of a contract series.
E at a waiting passenger: accept the ride. E again after stopping: drop them off.
F1: console. help: list of commands. Up/down arrows: history. Tab: command completion.
In the menu, 1–3 choose a slot, L loads the selected one. F5 saves to the active slot, F9 loads the selected one. Saving to another slot from the pause menu creates a copy. A new game and loading set the active slot. The game is saved every two minutes of play, on pause and on a normal exit.

Command examples: weapons, health, god on, spawn pills, money 1000, hour 7, rain on, wanted 3, recover.
N adds six hours, P toggles rain.
Hitting a pedestrian knocks them down, a fast hit is fatal; this raises the wanted level.
Bodies, wrecked and abandoned cars disappear once you are more than 245 m away; the city is replenished elsewhere.
A car sinks in deep water. Water puts out fire. Potholes toss the car up.

The population of a new game (low, medium, high, full, maximum) is chosen in settings and applies to the next new game.
Maximum is the default: 4000 cars and 14000 residents. Full is the population of the C# version: 1485 cars and 5265 residents. High is 1080 and 3240.
Launching with --density low|medium|high|full|max changes it for one session. A game already started keeps its density.
Pedestrians wait at traffic lights and walk around each other. A car standing without throttle is held by the brake.

The interface language (русский, English, українська) is chosen in settings.
Launching with --language ru|en|uk changes the language for one session.

Saves and the log: %LOCALAPPDATA%/PromzonaCpp (Windows), ~/Library/Application Support/PromzonaCpp (macOS), ~/.local/share/PromzonaCpp (Linux).
A save of the C# version is imported like this: Promzona.exe --import-save "path\save.json".
The source file is not changed; the result goes to the first free slot.
Imported are the city (seed), time, weather, money, health, weapons, wanted level and job counters; whoever was driving gets a car of the same kind. In a city of the old layout (revision 0, 49×49 grid) the player, traffic cars and pedestrians are put back in their places; in other cities the player starts at the usual spot and the city is populated anew, with the population chosen for a new game.
New saves use the promzona-cpp-7 format. The game also opens native saves of versions 5 and 6. Earlier versions require a new game.

In the main menu and the pause menu, F2 changes the graphics: anti-aliasing at 100% (default), 75%, or no processing at 100%. The interface is always at window resolution. The choice is saved. Settings offer a draw distance of 130–3000 m (1000 m by default), three detail levels, glow, weather and particle effects, master volume and radio.
