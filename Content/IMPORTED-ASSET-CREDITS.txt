PROMZONA 0.20.0 — external asset credits
========================================
The following downloaded models have been adapted for this game. Original source
and author credits are reproduced below. Each prepared model folder also contains
the exact SOURCE-LICENSE.txt supplied with its archive.
Changes: metre/axis normalization, reduced LOD geometry, resized texture maps,
rigid wheel/front-door segmentation on road vehicles, scene placement and collision proxies.
The high-detail VAZ has separately assigned glazing and reworked normals/LOD budgets.
The tyre scan has its original scanned ground removed.
The GAZ-52 uses its supplied Sketchfab Standard licence; it is not a CC asset.
The remaining fifteen models carry CC BY 4.0 attribution licences.
Original ZIP downloads are not overwritten. Do not distribute extracted assets
as an asset pack; review each supplied licence for downstream reuse.
